Changes: removed material/UV/normal fields, triangulated quad faces, compressed the geometry as numeric arrays, and added illustrative surface markers selected from manually set anatomical target positions. No shape blendshapes or shape-generation tools are included. The visible sensor markers are not calibrated physical locations or the research pipeline's joint-regressor argmax vertices. This sample is not asserted to be the exact male mean-shape body used for the paper's metrics.
